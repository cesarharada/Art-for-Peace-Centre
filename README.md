# :man_artist: Who is Tetsuo Harada?

Born in the ashes of post-war Japan, Tetsuo HARADA (1949-2024) was a Japanese monumental sculptor who dedicated his life to making art for peace. Here is a one-minute video showing his process in his atelier in France.

![image alt](https://github.com/cesarharada/Art-for-Peace-Centre/blob/d61afbd614824922090a330431ede924760d1c95/Images/Video.png)
https://youtu.be/Kw6ZHwRmuOg?si=06LvySJxnvmA-xQ0

For his dream of “Earth Weaving”, he created huge links of granite that would emerge from the ground, re-enter the earth, and re-appear in a different country—transforming the whole planet into one artwork, building the “internet of friendship”. In 1995, he built Japan’s largest sculpture: a decorated ecological dam that is a 300-ton, 120-meter-long granite sculpture.

# :peace_symbol: What does it have to do with Art for Peace?
Tetsuo HARADA passed away in Nov 2024 and left behind hundreds of sculptures and thousands of paintings and drawings. In the process of archiving and promoting his legacy of art for peace, we will develop workflows, tools, and infrastructure that can be useful for other artists, and for cultural institutions learning and collaborating with the best in the industry.

# :muscle: What are we going to do?

## :books: 1. Archive

With museum-grade calibration, we photograph, 2D scan, and 3D scan the spaces, sculptures, models, paintings, drawings, sketches, photos, texts, and files. We then clean the images, tag the files, and organise them into computer folder structures that make it easy to organise the data for human readability, and also for SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) and AI. Once all the images are stored in the original resolution as well as in smaller resolutions, the outputs will be:

- **On your local hard drive**, a simple folder neatly organised in sub-folders that are correctly named and accessible through an easy-to-use local offline app.
- **In the cloud**, you will be able to upload that same folder and choose what you want to share or conceal.
- **For long-term storage offline, we use M-disk**, which is a type of low-cost (5USD per 100 GB) that is guaranteed to last a thousand years and that can be engraved and read on a regular DVD writer / player device.

Having well-organised annotated data would enable artists to easily create websites, exhibition catalogues, books and catalogue raisonné with AI. We aim to build an open source platform that can improve from https://artlogic.net/ (for artists) and https://preservica.com/  (for archivists and library) based on state of the art standards such as https://www.dublincore.org/. 

**Art for Peace Archive**
We want to archive the most important artworks for peace in the world to survive WW3, and beyond. Not only Tetsuo Harada’s work, but over time, we will archive the most precious artworks for peace made by other artists. The entire body of work of Tetsuo Harada should fit on a single M-disc. Every year, we will add a few new artworks for peace from other artists. That will take the form of a 

- **Art for Peace Journal:** A4, pre-hole-punched selection of artworks for peace, that can be added to an acid-free binder.
- **Art for Peace Spindle:** a single M-Disc with new artworks for peace. It will contain the same artworks found in the A4 paper journal, with all the same metadata.

![image alt](https://github.com/cesarharada/Art-for-Peace-Centre/blob/547baa638fde0c34d1580fac538efef7d74f51fc/Images/Art%20for%20Peace%20Spindle.jpg)
Art collectors, libraries, and universities around the world can subscribe and receive updates to their local copy of the “Art for Peace Spindle”. This way, even in case of WW3 or WW4, we should always be able to find a copy of Art for Peace somewhere.

Future generations will find that, even in times of war, humans had a conscience, and collaborated to create and protect beauty and meaning together.
![image alt](https://github.com/cesarharada/Art-for-Peace-Centre/blob/547baa638fde0c34d1580fac538efef7d74f51fc/Images/Offline%20validator.png)

## :link: 2. Earth Weaving
![image alt](https://github.com/cesarharada/Art-for-Peace-Centre/blob/547baa638fde0c34d1580fac538efef7d74f51fc/Images/Harada%20World%20Map.png)

- **In green** are the locations where Tetsuo Harada has already installed sculptures accessible to the public. Mostly Western Europe and East Asia.
- **In red** are the locations suggested by peace researcher Seth Kinzie that are symbolic locations to build Art for Peace communities, and that are already of historical or symbolic importance.

We are looking for a transportation and logistics sponsor to help us install artworks. We could sell, lease, or donate artworks—from monumental granite sculptures to paintings to drawings—starting with those symbolic sites. Eventually, the ambition is to have one of Tetsuo Harada’s artworks in every country, accessible to the public, linking back to the website and promoting other artists who are also working for peace.

## :dove: **3. Art for Peace Centre**
![image alt](https://github.com/cesarharada/Art-for-Peace-Centre/blob/547baa638fde0c34d1580fac538efef7d74f51fc/Images/Centre%20Section%20Cut.png)

We will build an invisible museum that blends with nature. Inside an artificial mountain, we would create a hollow, lightweight structure and open the museum only during the sunlight hours. The museum would be built with durable materials, such as compressed earth, stainless steel, and glass, so that the museum can last thousands of years with minimal maintenance costs and remain energetically passive. The museum activities would revolve around:

- **Exhibitions**: Permanent and temporary collections
- **Event Space** for quiet and noisy gatherings
- A **shop** and a **cafe**
- **Workshops** open for the public activities
- **Library** and **archive** for research and education
- **Artist for Peace Residency** to support artists for peace ****
- **Annual Art for Peace Award**, that would determine the artwork on the cover of the journal and website.
- **Offices** and **meeting rooms** would serve the headquarters for the **community**, **journal** and production of the **spindles.**
- And of course **utilities** " toilets, lockers, maintenance, storage, data centre, fire safety, and others

We are still looking for the location, but ideally it would be within the Greater Paris area, reachable by public transport. Please reach out if you have some sites in mind. See this [book](https://librairiesanstitre.com/products/9782360890224).
